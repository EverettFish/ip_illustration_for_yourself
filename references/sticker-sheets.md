# Themed 3:4 sticker sheets

Use this route for 贴纸、贴纸页、异形贴纸、模切贴纸、kiss-cut sticker sheets, or sticker sets.

## Identity first

Reuse an accepted anchor. If the user supplied only a non-house-style character image, create and accept the anchor before the sheets. Write an exact fidelity lock for hair or fur silhouette, bangs, side locks, back length, ears or horns, eyes, face, proportions, outfit, accessories, and palette. Repeat it for every tiny scene and head-only sticker.

For multiple IPs, make one separate three-page set per IP unless the user explicitly asks for a crossover or group sheet.

## Default set

Unless changed by the user, create three exact 3:4 portrait sheets, one generation call per sheet:

1. `Life Stickers`: eating, drinking, rest, outings, hobbies, and playful reactions
2. `Work Stickers`: office actions, a removable simple suit layer, coffee, plain silver unbranded laptop, notebook, presentation, headphones
3. `Media Stickers`: heart-hands, thumbs-up, thanks, filming, editing, phone, microphone, ring light, hearts, share arrows

Use only the English thematic title in the header. Never put a personal name, account name, or handle there unless requested. Keep body text `NONE` by default, including Media. Communicate like, repost, and thanks visually.

## Page structure

- Use exact 3:4 portrait with two harmonious flat light-pastel zones.
- Reserve only the top 12–16% for one full-width illustrated header block with no packaging hole.
- Draw the exact series title as naive mouse lettering: visibly hand-positioned, gently wobbly, uneven stroke width, slight baseline drift, awkward but readable spacing.
- Run a continuous lawn, meadow, or theme-equivalent ground from the far left edge to the far right edge. Distribute grass, flowers, leaves, stones, and tiny relevant details across the width.
- Integrate one tiny accepted-IP scene directly into the header. The header is not a sticker: no white halo, cut border, floating contour, or separate panel around its title, character, or art.
- Use the lower 84–88% as one uninterrupted, harmonizing, texture-free sticker field.

## Default 18-piece field

Arrange exactly 18 independent body pieces unless the user specifies 10–20:

- 3 wide scene-led stickers with the IP visible inside the setting
- 5 full-body or half-body IP actions
- 4 complete-hair or complete-fur heads, faces, or expressive busts
- 6 very small theme-specific filler stickerlets

Every large or medium sticker must contain the IP or be an IP-led scene. Cameras, computers, food, drinks, office objects, and symbols may be standalone only in the smallest filler tier.

## Density and rhythm

- Target a combined cut-border footprint of about 75–82% of the sticker field.
- Keep each primary sticker modest, then run a density pass with six to eight micro fillers while keeping the total at or below 20.
- Stop when no empty pocket is much larger than one micro filler.
- Occupy upper, middle, side, and lower areas.
- Form two tidy visual rails with roughly equal side margins.
- Use a calm staggered diagonal or S-curve rhythm, compact even breathing gaps, stable upright reading, and gentle rotations only.
- Never use straight rows, uniform columns, a rigid grid, a separate prop strip, or bottom-loaded accents.
- Keep nearby pieces thematically related as loose micro-clusters without touching.

## Silhouettes and cut borders

- Give each body piece its own continuous white or very pale irregular kiss-cut border.
- Keep every cut contour fully visible and separate: no touching, overlap, merge, or crop.
- Vary silhouettes decisively: wide irregular scenes, tall or diagonal actions, compact full-hair heads, angular or elongated micro motifs.
- Avoid repeated ovals, rounded blobs, uniformly sized full bodies, cast shadows, glossy vinyl, blister packaging, mockup hands, or rectangular UI cards.
- Keep the illustration inside each border in the Mengli broken-pen and slightly misregistered flat-fill style.

## Context reuse

Inspect the conversation for one or two directly relevant products, places, colors, or motifs. Keep all contextual material below about 20% of the manifest and integrate it as a small scene detail, outfit accent, or micro prop. Supporting images never control style or identity. Skip irrelevant easter eggs, brands, slogans, and second characters.

## Manifest

Plan:

- exact theme and header title
- full-width header mini-scene
- exact total count
- 3 scene stickers with setting, action, props, scale, and silhouette
- 5 action stickers with pose, expression, outfit, prop, scale, and silhouette
- 4 complete-hair heads or busts
- 6 distributed micro fillers
- zero to two context elements and their exact location
- body text manifest, normally `NONE`
- dominant sheet color plus two to four anchor-derived accents

## Validate and repair

Check:

1. exact 3:4 ratio and shallow 12–16% full-width header
2. exact title, complete edge-to-edge ground, integrated IP, and no header cut border
3. 10–20 individually countable body pieces matching the manifest
4. 75–82% footprint with compact even gaps and no large void
5. tidy side rails, staggered rhythm, and no row, grid, or prop strip
6. strong scale and silhouette variation
7. separate complete cut border around every body piece
8. coherent scenes, actions, heads, and distributed micro fillers
9. every large/medium piece is IP-led and every prop-only piece is tiny
10. anchor identity in every appearance, including heads and tiny scenes
11. broken pen contours, loose flat-fill registration, and clean saturation
12. no body text unless explicitly locked

Use targeted repairs:

- `Remove every white halo and cut contour from the header; merge its title, tiny IP, and full-width ground into one continuous illustration.`
- `Shrink primary stickers 15–25%, separate every border, and restore compact even paper gaps.`
- `Redistribute pieces across upper, middle, sides, and lower areas until no void exceeds one micro filler.`
- `Break straight rows and the bottom prop strip; interleave the same pieces along one calm S-curve.`
- `Replace every large standalone prop with an IP-led action or scene; keep standalone props micro only.`
- `Diversify scene, action, head, and micro silhouettes; remove repeated rounded blobs.`
- `Restore the accepted hair/fur mass, bangs, ears, face, proportions, outfit cues, and palette in every appearance.`
- `Render exactly [COUNT] pieces; remove duplicates and add only the missing manifest item.`

Keep accepted pages and repair only the failing page.
